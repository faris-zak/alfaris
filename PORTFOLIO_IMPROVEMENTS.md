## Suggested implementation phases

1. **Verify and edit.** Start with UTAS, candidacy wording, VitalX, existing artifact links and the early awards. These changes can stand on their own. Collect missing records in parallel. Share the revised copy with the author for accuracy.
2. **Improve the recruiter journey.** Publish the first two case studies, revise the section order and mobile entry, and provide the direct CV route. Check a narrow viewport, keyboard navigation and a fresh CV open. Preserve the works cited in the audit as candid nominations, participation and former roles.
3. **Develop the academic evidence.** Add a dated study artifact when ready. Concentrate further visual work on obstacles observed in the reading test. Defer purely decorative changes until they solve a specific problem.

### How to judge the result

Suggested acceptance exercise, not a claim about current visitor behavior: give three first-time readers one task each, with no coaching. Give the software reviewer 30 seconds to identify a shipped change, its author and an accessible artifact. Give the aerospace reviewer 30 seconds to find the simulator or VitalX and distinguish modeled behavior from a demonstrated outcome. Give the academic reviewer 60 seconds to find a question, method, result and limitation, or accurately identify that the study is still in progress. Then ask each to locate a CV and a contact route. Record the first missed step and where the reader looked. Fix that before adding another section.
